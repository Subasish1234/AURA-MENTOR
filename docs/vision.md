# AURA Vision

AURA is an AI companion that grows with students from their first semester to their first job.

The goal is to combine:

* LLMs
* RAG
* Agents
* Knowledge Graphs
* Recommendation Systems
* Voice AI
* Generative AI
* Personalized Memory

into a single platform that provides academic, research, project, and career assistance.

AURA should feel like a mentor, friend, coach, researcher, and collaborator rather than a traditional chatbot.
